Zode home list, redesigned

Today every conversation is the same card, and three of them are called "shop-api". This puts what needs you first with the buttons on it, names conversations by what they are about, and packs history into rows you can scan and swipe.

Zode

What changed, and why

  1. 1
    A small header and no headline.Wordmark, Mac connection chip, search and menu, then the list starts. Needs you sits first, so the answer to "does anything need me" is the first thing on screen.
  2. 2
    Conversations are named by their task.The row leads with what it is about ("Fix flaky checkout test") and the project sits underneath, so three conversations in one project stop looking identical.
  3. 3
    Needs you carries the answer.The command or question is on the card, with Allow, Deny or Try again. No trip into the chat to find out what it wants.
  4. 4
    History is a grouped list, not a stack of cards.One surface per section with hairlines, 56 pt rows and times in one column. About twice as many conversations fit on screen.
  5. 5
    Filter and act where you are.Project chips and search narrow the list. Swipe a row left to archive, with Undo. Anything older than yesterday folds into one line.
