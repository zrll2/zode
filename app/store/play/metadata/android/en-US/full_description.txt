Zode is the phone companion for ZCode, the AI coding app. Start a task in ZCode on your Mac, then keep an eye on it from the couch, the kitchen, or the train.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See what needs you. Sessions waiting for your answer sit at the top. Running ones come next. Finished ones are grouped by day.
• Read the work like a document. Text, code and tables look clean, and tool steps fold into one quiet line.
• Reply, stop, or start. Answer a question, stop a task that went the wrong way, or start a new one in the folder you choose.
• Catch up after a bad connection. Zode shows what you missed.
• Light and dark look that follow your phone.

HOW IT CONNECTS
Zode is a companion, so ZCode keeps running on your Mac. A small free relay on the same Mac passes messages between your phone and ZCode:
Phone  ->  Zode relay on your Mac  ->  ZCode
Install the relay with one command, then pair once by scanning a QR code. At home Zode uses your Wi-Fi. Away from home, use a private network such as Tailscale.

BEFORE YOU START
You need ZCode on a Mac and the free, open-source Zode relay: https://github.com/zrll2/zode

NO MAC NEARBY?
Tap "Try the demo" on the first screen to explore everything with sample data. No account or network needed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your messages go between your phone and your Mac only. We have no servers.
