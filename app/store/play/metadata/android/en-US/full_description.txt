Zode is a companion app for ZCode, the AI coding app. It lets you follow and control your ZCode coding sessions from your phone. Your Mac stays the host: it runs ZCode and a small free relay that bridges your phone to ZCode.

WHAT YOU NEED
1. ZCode installed on a Mac.
2. The free, open-source Zode relay running on that same Mac (set up with one command: https://github.com/zrll2/zode).
3. This app on your phone.

HOW IT CONNECTS
Phone  ->  Zode relay on your Mac  ->  ZCode
You pair your phone once by scanning a QR code that the relay shows on your Mac. After that, Zode reaches your Mac over your home network, or over a private network such as Tailscale when you are away. Nothing goes through our servers, because there are none.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See at a glance what needs you, what is running, and what finished, grouped by day.
• Read the agent's work like a document: Markdown, code blocks, tables, and tool calls folded into one quiet line.
• Send a message, stop a running turn, or start a new session in a folder you choose.
• Catch up after a dropped connection. Zode replays what you missed.
• Light and dark appearance that follows your phone.

NO MAC HANDY?
Tap "Try the demo" on the first screen to explore the whole app with sample data. No account or network needed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Pairing uses a one-time code, and your phone keeps its own revocable token in the secure store.
