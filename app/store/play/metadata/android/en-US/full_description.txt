Zode is the phone companion for the ZCode desktop app on your Mac.

Your coding agent works on your Mac. Zode lets you follow it from the sofa, the train or the next room.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See at a glance what needs you, what is running, and what finished, grouped by day.
• Read the agent's work like a document: Markdown, code blocks, tables, and tool calls folded into one quiet line.
• Send a message, stop a running turn, or start a new session in a folder you choose.
• Catch up after a dropped connection. Zode replays what you missed.
• Light and dark appearance that follows your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
Zode talks to a small open-source relay that runs on your own Mac. You pair your phone once by scanning a QR code. Nothing goes through our servers, because there are none. Your conversations stay between your phone and your Mac.

NO MAC HANDY?
Tap "Try the demo" on the first screen to explore the whole app with sample data.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Pairing uses a one-time code, and your phone keeps its own revocable token in the Android Keystore-backed secure store.
• Use Tailscale or another private network to reach your Mac when you are away from home.

SETUP
Zode needs the free open-source Zode relay on a Mac. Instructions: https://github.com/zrll2/zode
