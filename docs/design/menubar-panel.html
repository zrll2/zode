Zode menu bar, redesigned

The native menu is a 27-row list. This replaces it with one small panel that answers three questions in order: does anything need me, what is running, what finished.

What changed, and why

Quiet

Needs you
  1. 1
    The icon answers first.A small amber dot means something is waiting on you, green means work is running. Most of the time you never open the panel.
  2. 2
    Things that need you come first, with the buttons.An approval shows the exact command and Allow or Deny right there. A stalled task offers Nudge or Stop. No submenus to hunt through.
  3. 3
    Running and finished are quiet.Times sit in one right-aligned column and finished work folds away. Older results hide behind one line.
  4. 4
    Settings leave the main view.Relay, address, login, awake, updates and Quit move behind the gear. The phone count in the footer opens pairing.
  5. 5
    It is a real panel, not a menu.A native menu cannot align text, hold buttons or show a progress dot. A small window under the icon can, and the right-click menu stays as a fallback.
